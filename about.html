I'm a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA, where I work on chiplet-based LLM accelerators built with advanced packaging, and on the security of those systems.

I completed my M.S. in Computer Science & Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, specialising in hardware security (GPA 9.68/10). Along the way I worked on side-channel attacks at IISc, deep-learning side-channel analysis at the University of Florida, and entropy estimation and SHA-3 at Qualcomm.

Outside the lab you'll find me strength training, swimming, or debating.
